How to play

Dueply

Two real-world numbers go head to head. Card A shows its number and Card B keeps its number hidden. Work out how B compares.

Two modes

Daily Challenge

Everyone gets the same 10 duels , and a new set arrives at midnight UTC. Each round scores up to 100 points, so the maximum is 1000. At the end you see how your score compares with an average player, and you can copy your share grid.

Infinite Arcade

Endless duels from the full database, and no card repeats in a run. Each correct answer moves card B into card A's place and adds 1 to your streak. One miss ends the run, and your personal best is saved.

Two kinds of round

Higher / Lower

Is B’s number higher or lower than A’s? In the Daily, a correct call scores 100 points and a miss scores 0.

Scale Shot

How many times bigger or smaller is B than A? Set your guess with the slider or type a number. The closer your guess, the more points you get. A perfect guess scores 100 and a guess 10× away scores 0. In the Arcade, landing within 2.5× keeps your streak alive.
